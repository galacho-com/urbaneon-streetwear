URBANEON

Modern streetwear for bold city living.

URBANEON is a contemporary streetwear ecommerce concept built around
urban apparel, sneakers, accessories, and everyday essentials for modern
city life.

The brand combines bold visual identity, clean product presentation, and
a fast digital shopping experience designed for customers who want
everyday pieces with a distinctive streetwear attitude.

Brand Positioning

URBANEON focuses on contemporary urban fashion with an emphasis on
versatile pieces, sneakers, accessories, and statement essentials.

than a generic clothing catalog.

📸 Product Photography Standards

Product photography is a central part of the URBANEON experience.

Every product image should follow a consistent standard:

Realistic photography

Clean professional presentation

Full product visibility

Correct proportions

Strong but controlled lighting

No unnecessary cropping

No screenshots of ecommerce websites

No prices embedded into images

No buttons or UI elements inside images

No ratings baked into photography

No promotional copy embedded in product images

No unrelated products in a product frame

Correct image assigned to the correct product

Product cards should prioritize clear, attractive product photography,
while editorial sections may use lifestyle photography.
